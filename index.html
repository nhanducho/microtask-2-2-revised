Which flows do you observe?

When you order a pair of running shoes online, three flows move through the same supply chain. They happen at the same time, often in different directions, and depend on one another.

Material flow

Materials and products move between locations.

Information flow

Orders and data coordinate what should happen.

Financial flow

Payments and invoices connect supply chain participants.

Key takeaway

One network, three connected flows

The flows happen at the same time, often move in different directions, and depend on one another.

Material flow

Materials and products move through factories, warehouses, transport links and customer locations.

Information flow

Orders and data coordinate what should be produced, stored, shipped and delivered.

Financial flow

Payments and invoices compensate the participants that supply the product.

Connected, not separate: the parcel cannot reach the customer without the right information, and the network cannot keep operating without financial flows. Material flow is also commonly called physical flow.
